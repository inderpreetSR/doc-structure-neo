# Data Module ITM-ORG-005 — 36-role register and career progression

**Issue:** 001  
**Information code:** Role identification / functional taxonomy  
**Count control:** 36 roles; 5 groups. Counts describe functions, not staffing.

A hierarchy is illustrative and ascending in scope. An arrow (→) shows a possible career progression, **not a claim that every role reports to the next title**. Regional grading systems and job titles vary.

## Interpretation requirements

1. **Role:** Set of duties performed by a person.
2. **Team:** Delivery or operations group containing people and roles.
3. **Function/Department:** Organisation unit that may comprise several teams.
4. **Service owner:** Accountable party for a particular production service.
5. **Approver:** Person or group authorised to accept a class of change/risk; not necessarily the implementation owner.

**Senior individual-contributor track:** Engineer → Senior Engineer → Staff/Principal → Distinguished, where supported.  
**People-management track:** Team Lead (where managerial) → Manager → Senior Manager → Director → VP/Head.

Do not equate **SRE support levels (L1/L2/L3)** with corporate job grade L1/L2/L3.
